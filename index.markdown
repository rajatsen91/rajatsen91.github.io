I am a Staff Research Scientist at Google Research. I am excited about structured data foundation models and making + deploying them in real enterprise problems. Nowadays I spend most of my time thinking about the impending seismic shift in structured data prediction pipelines. I am one of the creators of [TimesFM](https://github.com/google-research/timesfm) and also the research lead of [TabFM](https://github.com/google-research/tabfm).

I obtained my PhD from UT Austin in 2019 where I was fortunate to be advised by [Dr. Sanjay Shakkottai](http://users.ece.utexas.edu/~shakkott/Sanjay_Shakkottai/Contact.html). I also had the pleasure of working closely with [Dr. Alex Dimakis](http://users.ece.utexas.edu/~dimakis/), during my time at UT. I have worked on contextual bandits, extreme classification and black-box optimization.



## Updates

**[Sep 24, 2026]** Our paper [Rethinking Post-Training Recipes for Multimodal Time-Series Forecasting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.29401), on post-training LLMs to revise TimesFM forecasts using multimodal context, has been accepted to NeurIPS 2026.\\
**[Aug 31, 2026]** We released [TimesFM-3](https://research.google/blog/timesfm-3-a-zero-shot-foundation-model-for-multivariate-forecasting/), our next-generation zero-shot foundation model natively pre-trained for multivariate time-series forecasting.\\
**[Jun 30, 2026]** We released [TabFM](https://research.google/blog/introducing-tabfm-a-zero-shot-foundation-model-for-tabular-data/), a zero-shot foundation model for tabular data classification and regression.\\
**[Apr 30, 2026]** Our paper [Rethinking Multimodal Time-Series Forecasting Evaluation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.06973), introducing the TimesX multimodal time-series forecasting benchmark, has been accepted to ICML 2026.
